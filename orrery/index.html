        How to use ASTROVIEW

        • Select a body by clicking it, or use Search and Filters in the dock.
        • Rotate by dragging. Zoom with Ctrl (⌘ on Mac) + scroll, or pinch on touch. Scrolling alone always moves the page. Reset view is in Controls.
        • Compare any two planets from the Compare drawer, or press Compare on a planet’s card.
        • Tour flies you through every body with a short description.
        • Planet stories ↓ scrolls to the cinematic sections below the solar system.
        • Keys: P pause · ← → previous or next body · R reset · Esc closes a drawer

        Couldn’t load the 3D library

        Check your internet connection and refresh. For offline use, follow the README and put Three.js in the libs folder.
